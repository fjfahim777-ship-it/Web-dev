# বাজার দর (Bazar Dor)

Bazar Dor is a responsive Bengali-language web application that helps users explore the prices of essential products, compare market prices, track daily price changes, and view product details in one place.

## 🚀 Technologies Used

* Next.js
* React
* TypeScript
* Tailwind CSS
* DaisyUI
* Better Auth
* React-Toastify

## ✨ Features

### 1. Explore Essential Products

Browse essential products with their names, units, and current prices displayed in Bengali numerals.

### 2. Track Daily Price Changes

Explore products whose prices have increased or decreased to get a quick overview of daily market changes.

### 3. Browse Products by Category

Explore products by category and sort them by price, from lowest to highest or highest to lowest.

### 4. View Product Details

View detailed product information, including minimum, maximum, and average prices, along with market-wise prices for individual products.

### 5. Authentication and Profile Management

Create an account or sign in using email and password, Google, or GitHub. Users can also update their profile information and sign out.

### 6. Responsive Interface

The application is designed to work smoothly across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

## 📌 Project Overview

Bazar Dor is designed to provide a simple and convenient way to explore the prices of essential products. With category-based browsing, daily price changes, and market-wise price information, users can check product prices at a glance through a Bengali-language interface.
